How to Send a Funeral Wreath or Funeral Bouquet to Tallinn from Abroad

6 October 2026

A funeral wreath or funeral bouquet can be ordered from abroad and delivered directly to a funeral ceremony, church, chapel, cemetery, crematorium, family home or another agreed address in Tallinn or Harju County.

If you live abroad and cannot attend a funeral in Estonia in person, you can still send flowers on your behalf. The arrangement is prepared locally and delivered to the agreed place at the agreed time.

This is especially useful when family members, relatives, friends or colleagues live in another country but would still like to send a respectful floral tribute to Tallinn or Harju County.

How does ordering funeral flowers from abroad work?

The process is simple. Choose a funeral wreath or funeral bouquet, send the delivery details and your contact information, and the florist prepares the arrangement locally in Tallinn. You do not need to arrange transport or ask someone in Estonia to collect the flowers for you.

Where can a funeral wreath or funeral bouquet be delivered?

Funeral flowers can be delivered directly to the place where the farewell or funeral ceremony takes place. Delivery is also possible to a private home or another agreed address when the flowers are intended for the family rather than the ceremony itself.

Typical delivery locations include:

  • funeral ceremonies;
  • churches and chapels;
  • cemeteries;
  • crematoriums and funeral halls;
  • private homes;
  • another agreed address in Tallinn or Harju County.

Common funeral locations in Tallinn

If the funeral takes place at a cemetery, chapel or crematorium, include the exact name and address with the order. Common funeral and farewell locations in Tallinn include:

  • Pärnamäe Cemetery and Tallinn Crematorium – Pärnamäe tee 36, Tallinn
  • Liiva Cemetery – Kalmistu tee 34, Tallinn
  • Rahumäe Cemetery – Rahumäe tee 8, Tallinn
  • Metsakalmistu Cemetery – Kloostrimetsa tee 36, Tallinn
  • Siselinna Cemetery – Toonela tee 3, Tallinn
  • Siselinna Funeral Hall – Toonela tee 7, Tallinn
  • Pirita Cemetery – Rummu tee 7a, Tallinn
  • Hiiu-Rahu Cemetery – Hiiu-Suurtüki 1, Tallinn

Delivery in Harju County

Funeral wreaths and funeral bouquets can also be delivered to ceremonies in Harju County. Common destinations near Tallinn include Jüri, Maardu and Randvere, as well as other churches, cemeteries, chapels and agreed addresses in the area.

  • Jüri Cemetery – Kiriku tn 1, Jüri, Rae Parish
  • Maardu Cemetery – Keemikute tn 1b, Maardu
  • Randvere Cemetery – Kirikaia tee 1, Randvere, Viimsi Parish

If the ceremony takes place somewhere else in Harju County, the delivery possibility can be confirmed before the order is finalised.

Funeral wreath or funeral bouquet?

A funeral wreath is a formal and traditional choice for a funeral ceremony, church, chapel or cemetery. A funeral bouquet is smaller and can be suitable both for a ceremony and for delivery to a family home or another private address.

If you are unsure which option is more suitable, you can choose according to the type of ceremony, your relationship to the deceased and the size of the floral tribute you would like to send.
